### Charlie Hubbard

**[charliehubbard.org](https://charliehubbard.org)**

Information Systems and Statistics & Machine Learning at Carnegie Mellon.

**Research**
- [The Hand Stays Cold](https://github.com/CharlieHubbard7/college_golf): does a bad hole carry over in college golf? 1.58M holes, Clippd data, advised by Ron Yurko.
- [NFL career value](https://github.com/CharlieHubbard7/NFLResearch): when players peak vs. when teams pay them, from 25 years of Madden ratings.
- [The Bounce-Back Effect](https://github.com/CharlieHubbard7/golf_hot_hand): on the PGA Tour, birdies are followed by slightly worse holes, not more birdies.

**Markets and systems**
- [matching-engine](https://github.com/CharlieHubbard7/matching-engine): a C++20 order book that handles 16M msgs/sec on replayed Nasdaq order flow.
- [PGA-Market-Data](https://github.com/CharlieHubbard7/PGA-Market-Data): live Kalshi and Polymarket golf markets next to the leaderboard and a model.
- [Bettor](https://github.com/CharlieHubbard7/Bettor): a betting paper-trader that measures itself by closing line value.

**Learning agents**
- [2048-rl-sandbox](https://github.com/CharlieHubbard7/2048-rl-sandbox): play 2048 next to N-tuple, DQN and PPO agents in the browser.
- [flappy-neat-sandbox](https://github.com/CharlieHubbard7/flappy-neat-sandbox): watch 50 networks learn Flappy Bird with NEAT.
